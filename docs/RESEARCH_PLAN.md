# Research Plan

## Proposed study concept

**Dynamic evaluation of 3D smile designs using a photorealistic 4D virtual patient.**

## Primary engineering question

Can a dynamic facial avatar be fused with metrically accurate intraoral geometry so that alternative dental CAD designs remain spatially stable and visually plausible throughout facial movement?

## Validation domains

1. **Registration accuracy** — landmark/mesh distances against a reference.
2. **Temporal stability** — frame-to-frame dental pose drift.
3. **Dental visibility accuracy** — predicted vs manually measured tooth display.
4. **Rendering realism** — blinded clinician ratings.
5. **Design comparison repeatability** — same motion, different CAD designs.
6. **Computation** — reconstruction time and rendering FPS.

## Suggested first paper

*A Dynamic Dental Gaussian Avatar for Motion-Aware 3D Smile Design: Development and Proof-of-Concept.*
